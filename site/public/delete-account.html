Delete your account

You can delete your Parallax account yourself, at any time, from the phone app, the desktop app, or the web. It takes effect at once and can't be undone.

Before you delete

Remove your computers first. A computer stays connected to Parallax Connect with its own key after the account is gone. Until you remove it, it keeps sending notifications to the account's phones, receiving its webhooks, serving guest links, and reaching the account's other computers.

  • In the desktop app, open Settings > Connections > Computers and choose Remove on each computer.
  • The app won't remove the computer you're on. Run plx connect remove there.
  • To keep a computer registered but disconnected, turn off Reach from anywhere in its Settings > Connections, or run plx connect unlink on it.

From the phone app

  1. Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Under Security, tap Delete next to Delete account, then Continue.
  3. On the account page that opens, choose Delete account and confirm by typing Delete account.

The app signs out and says your account was deleted.

From the desktop app

  1. Open the profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Manage account.
  2. Under Security, choose Delete account. Your account page opens in the browser.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm by typing Delete account.

From the web, without the app

  1. Open accounts.parallax.codes/user/security and sign in.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm by typing Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address, name, picture, sign-in methods, password, passkeys, and sessions.
  • Your sign-in on every device. Each app signs out the next time it checks, within a day.

What is kept

  • Everything on your computers. Your threads, code, and files never left them, and deleting your account doesn't touch them. Uninstall Parallax from a computer to remove its data there.
  • The relay's record of computers you didn't remove, and their connections. Parallax Connect doesn't yet learn that an account was deleted. It keeps the account's list of computer ids, each computer's name and public key, and your phones' push tokens, and each computer stays connected and keeps working as "Before you delete" describes. A sign-in issued before the deletion keeps working at the relay for up to a day. After that no app can list or open those computers through the relay.
  • The ids of computers you removed, on the account's list of removed computers.
  • Push tokens, until Apple or Google retires them.
  • Usage counts from the desktop app, which carry a hash of the account's id and no name or email address. The hash is a pseudonym, not anonymous.
