Report a problem

Parallax is open source. Search the issues on GitHub for your problem, and open a new one if it isn't there. Say which app and version you use, the operating system, and what you did before it went wrong. In the phone app, Settings > About shows the version and has a connection log you can copy from.

Issues are public. Leave out anything private, such as code, tokens, or setup codes.
